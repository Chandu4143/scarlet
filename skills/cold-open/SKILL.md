# Cold open

A cold open puts a moment from later in the video at the very start, so the viewer knows within seconds why to keep watching. Which moment opens the video is the editor's call. Find the strongest options and let them choose.

## What makes a candidate

- It works with no setup. A viewer who has seen nothing yet understands it, or is curious about exactly the part they don't understand.
- It promises something the video delivers, without giving the payoff away: a surprising result, a strong claim, a reaction, a question, the moment something works or breaks.
- It is short, usually a single line or a few seconds of action. It starts on the first word or frame of the moment and ends on a button: the end of a sentence, a reaction, a beat that leaves the question open.
- When the moment needs the picture to land, such as a result appearing on screen, the picture is there in the open too.

## Offering it

Offer up to three candidates that differ in kind, such as a claim, a result and a reaction, each as an alternative the editor can play in place, strongest first. Nothing is applied until they choose. Each one is the moment copied to the very start of the edit across every synchronized track, followed by the video as it was. Its one sentence says what the moment is and why it pulls the viewer in.

When the editor names the moment, or only one moment works, make that one.

Leave the moment in its place later in the video as well, unless the editor asks to move it. The viewer recognizes it when they get there. Add no title, music or effect to the open unless asked.

`timeline_transcript`, `transcript_search`, `frame_contact_sheet`, `timeline_propose` and `timeline_join_audit` cover what this needs.
